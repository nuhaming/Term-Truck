TermTrack keeps your agreements in one searchable place, finds the dates and notice periods hidden in them, and gives each obligation an owner and a reminder.

1.Where your agreements live

In email, on laptops, in paper folders, and in shared drives. Important dates sit in long documents, notice periods slip by, and when the deadline comes nobody is sure whose job it was. Finding the latest signed version takes an afternoon.

2.What TermTrack does

  1. Stores agreements securely and organizes them by category, department, counterparty, and status.
  2. Finds contracts by party name, date, type, or keyword.
  3. Suggests parties, dates, renewal terms, and key clauses, then waits for a person to confirm.
  4. Reminds the responsible person before renewals, notice periods, and payment milestones.
  5. Assigns every obligation to a person or a team.
  6. Keeps a version history and limits who can view, download, or edit sensitive agreements.
Reminders show their status, so you can see if one failed.

3.How it works

  1. Create a workspace and invite authorized staff.
  2. Upload a contract, or enter the record by hand.
  3. Review the suggested details next to the source text.
  4. Confirm them and assign an owner.
  5. Receive reminders before each deadline.
  6. Update the status and record the action taken.
